# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). By taking part in it (issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space) you agree to follow it.

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:** be kind and patient, especially with people new to trading APIs or to open source; give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the community; own your mistakes and learn from them.

**Don't:** use sexualised language or imagery; troll, insult or make personal or political attacks; harass anyone in public or private; publish anyone's private information (including broker account details) without their explicit permission; or do anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

This project is also not a place to give or ask for investment advice or trading tips.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers through GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/MrChartist/api-bridge/security/advisories/new) (choose it even for conduct reports; it reaches the maintainers only), or contact a maintainer directly on GitHub. Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
