## Broker names and logos are not under the project licence

The dashboard shows each broker's logo (`server/bridge/dashboard/brokers/*.png`, one per supported broker), and the documentation, screenshots and code name the brokers. These names and logos are trademarks or brand marks of their owners. The licence of this repository does not apply to them and gives you no right to use them. They are here only to identify each broker's service so you can recognise it, and the project is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any broker. If you redistribute this project, the logos stay the property of their owners; remove the PNG files to ship without them (the dashboard then shows initials). A broker that would rather not appear can ask through an issue and its logo will be replaced with initials. See also `server/bridge/dashboard/brokers/README.md`.

Market data that a broker or a data source delivers to you stays under that provider's own terms, not under this licence.
